## Download

Pick your system, then an edition. Nothing else to install: Python and
everything Pythonic needs are inside.

| System | Pythonic | Pythonic + AI |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `…-windows-x64.zip` | `…-windows-x64-ai.zip` |
| macOS 14+ (Apple Silicon) | `…-macos-arm64.dmg` | `…-macos-arm64-ai.dmg` |
| Linux (64-bit) | `…-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | `…-linux-x86_64-ai.tar.gz` |

**Pythonic + AI** adds the AI drum generator (a larger download, about
1 GB). Without it everything else works the same.

### Windows
Unzip, open the `Pythonic` folder and double-click **Pythonic.exe**. The
first time, Windows may say it protected your PC: click **More info** ▸
**Run anyway**.

### macOS
Open the `.dmg` and drag **Pythonic** to **Applications**. The first time,
macOS says it cannot check the app: open **System Settings ▸ Privacy &
Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Pythonic.

### Linux
Extract the archive and run `Pythonic/Pythonic` (double-click it in your
file manager, or start it from a terminal).

The first start takes a few extra seconds while the sound engine compiles;
later starts are quick.
